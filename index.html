How Does Selfplus AGE+ Work?

Paragraph Its Relationship With Oxidative Stress
One of the main scientific interests surrounding resveratrol is its relationship with oxidative stress. Free radicals are reactive molecules produced during ordinary metabolic processes and through exposure to environmental factors. The body naturally maintains antioxidant defense systems to help regulate these molecules. When reactive molecules exceed the body's ability to manage them, oxidative stress may occur. This process has been investigated in connection with several biological and age-related conditions.

Paragraph Research Into Cellular Signaling Pathways
Researchers have also examined resveratrol's relationship with cellular signaling pathways, including pathways involving AMPK and SIRT1. These biological systems participate in processes related to energy regulation, metabolism, and cellular responses to environmental conditions. Because some of these pathways are associated with aging research, resveratrol has attracted considerable scientific attention. Studies involving cells and animals have produced interesting findings, but the human evidence is more complicated.

AGE+ is part of the SelfPlus line of health and wellness supplements — a once-daily resveratrol formula positioned around antioxidant support, cellular wellness, and a healthy-aging lifestyle. Each bottle contains 30 capsules, providing approximately a 30-day supply when used as directed. 

What is resveratrol?

Resveratrol is a naturally occurring plant compound and antioxidant produced by certain plants in response to environmental stress. It belongs to a group of compounds called polyphenols and is naturally found in sources such as red grapes, peanuts, certain berries, and Japanese knotweed. 

Where does the resveratrol in AGE+ come from?

The resveratrol in AGE+ is derived from Japanese Knotweed (Polygonum cuspidatum) root. Each capsule provides 250 mg of Japanese Knotweed root containing 50% Trans-Resveratrol, corresponding to 125 mg of Trans-Resveratrol.
